Stage26: frozen-checkpoint linear coverage probes, 2026-11-10
Purpose: localize recurring severe extent undercoverage without presupposing a new module.
Source models: Stage22 seed42 and Stage24 seed43, frozen and finetune34 best-Dice checkpoints as already selected. Never reselect their checkpoints.
Data: unchanged 1886 training / 762 development validation. No held-out test data or scores.
Readouts: encoder f3, encoder f4, decoder coarsest Swin block output, decoder final Swin block output. All are area-pooled to 8x8, then use a channel-linear logistic readout shared across positions.
Targets: exact 8x8 foreground occupancy (area average of the binary 256x256 training annotation).
Only new readout parameters train. All source encoder and decoder parameters and buffers must remain unchanged.
Feature standardization uses training mean/std only. No augmentation for this diagnostic probe. Initialize weights to zero and bias to training foreground log odds. L2=1e-4 on standardized weights, no penalty on intercept.
Optimization: deterministic full-batch float64 L-BFGS, strong Wolfe, up to 1000 iterations (50 calls with max_iter=20). Stop on max absolute objective gradient <=1e-5. Save all training-only diagnostics; do not inspect validation during optimization. A nonconverged probe is reported and cannot support a clean negative conclusion.
Probe output size and penalty are matched at the same feature location across frozen/fine and seeds. Different feature locations have different channel counts, so comparisons between locations are descriptive, not capacity-matched.
Validation primary diagnostic: 8x8 occupancy mean squared error and per-image absolute/signed area error. Supplement: 256x256 upsampled-logit masks, threshold 0.5, standard macro/pooled Dice/IoU, precision/recall, Prec@ thresholds, threshold Jaccard, BF1 and HD95.
Controls: archived full decoder predictions, training-only spatial foreground prior, and per-image all-foreground Dice reference. Never deploy GT-derived threshold or mask. The three recurrent IDs 305/581/791 are prespecified descriptive cases; all 762 cases and fixed GT area quartiles remain reported.
Verification: exact split/data audit, checkpoint/source hashes, original prediction reproduction at archived batch size 64, source model hash before/after, synthetic linear soft-target recovery, cached feature dimensions, convergence, no test read. Pipeline serializes smoke then full run.
A successful probe is a witness of accessible information, not proof that adding this probe as an auxiliary head will improve the full model. A failed linear probe is not proof of information absence. These development analyses cannot establish test generalization or paper novelty.
