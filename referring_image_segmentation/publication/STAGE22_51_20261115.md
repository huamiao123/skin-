# Experiment archive update: stages 22–51

This snapshot adds the later MambaVision experiments from `stage22_partial_finetune` through `stage51_spatial_gate_lr`, the separate `stage22_hiram_background_supervision_seed42` experiment, and related research notes. It preserves original protocols, source, logs, status, and numerical results as found on 2026-11-15; individual status and decision files determine whether each run completed and what conclusions are supported.

Start with [Stage 28](../stage28_drop_path/DECISION.json), [Stage 51 results](../stage51_spatial_gate_lr/RESULTS_zh.txt), and the [file inventory](stage22_51_source_inventory_20261115.json). Research notes are under `research_202611/`. The two stage 22 directories describe different experiments.

The archive excludes datasets, checkpoints, predictions, images, caches, and downloaded third-party source trees. Original paths and environment references remain historical and may need remapping for reproduction; availability of a metric or test evaluation must be checked in each experiment, and this archive does not assert that every stage underwent an independent test.
