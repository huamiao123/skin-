Stage27: training-only deep-feature coverage supervision, seed42, 2026-11-10
Problem/evidence: Stage26 readouts, trained on 1886 training images only, found usable extent evidence at encoder f4 in three recurrent severe undercoverage cases in both source seeds. The same f4 probe improved their masks in all six observations, but performed much worse overall than the full decoder. This is a witness of available information, not a causal proof and not validation of a new module.
Hypothesis: explicit coarse foreground occupancy supervision during adaptation may preserve useful extent evidence and make it easier for the full decoder to use it.
Arms: control and coverage_aux. Both use identical official MambaVision-S pretraining, original Swin decoder initialization, and train encoder stages3/4 plus decoder. Stages1/2 and stem stay frozen; encoder stays eval to keep BN/dropout policy fixed.
Only changed factor: coverage_aux adds 0.2 * BCEWithLogits on a 1x1 768-to-1 readout of f4 at 8x8. Targets are the binary training annotation averaged over each 32x32 cell. The coefficient is fixed once; no sweep. Original segmentation BCE+softDice remains exactly unchanged (including its original grayscale mask input).
Control contains the same zero-initialized 769-parameter auxiliary head but freezes and never invokes it. Thus shared weights/RNG/main outputs start identical; control optimizer skips its absent gradients. Candidate trains the auxiliary head. Normal evaluation skips this head in both arms.
Shared settings inherited from Stage22: 1886 train / 762 development / 867 held-out; no test evaluation. Seed42, 256x256, effective batch64, original flips and rotations, 300 epochs, encoder LR1e-4, decoder LR1e-3, AdamW wd0.01, original cosine schedule, original clip norm, BF16 forward and FP32 loss/parameters. Keep cached frozen prefix and input geometry unchanged.
Primary selection: each arm's maximum validation macro foreground Dice, earliest tie, with best-loss and last checkpoints retained. Full common evaluation metrics and per-image records for every criterion. Primary validation loss excludes the auxiliary objective in both arms; training total losses differ by definition.
Additional prespecified analysis: fixed area quartiles, recurrent IDs305/581/791, severe undercoverage recall<0.5, severe per-image Dice regressions, and corrections versus newly introduced errors on all762. These secondary analyses do not replace the overall metric or trigger per-case checkpoint selection.
Interpretation: improving only the three named cases while worsening overall performance does not establish a useful general improvement. This is an ordinary deep-supervision control, not claimed novelty. Failure also informs whether strengthening f4 alone is insufficient.
Checks: initial shared outputs/hash identical; synthetic target/gradient tests; all frozen prefix tensors unchanged; both late encoder stages train; auxiliary gradients present only in candidate; resume state exact; same established selective-scan backward tolerance (relative update L2<=1%, max abs parameter replay difference<=1e-4), no claim of bitwise encoder-backward determinism.
Smoke pipeline: one epoch, resume to second, then fresh formal initialization after smoke completion. Source/protocol/decision hashes fixed before formal. Preserve earlier stages and diagnostic failures. All writes on server only.

Post-run: evaluate the training-only head at the already selected candidate best-Dice checkpoint, as a secondary diagnostic. Do not select a separate auxiliary checkpoint or use its scores as the primary method. Control and candidate both start from fresh official pretraining, not trained Stage26/Stage22 weights.

Gradient isolation: original decoder parameters and encoder parameters keep their original separate norm1 clipping. The auxiliary head has its own AdamW group at LR1e-3 and separate norm1 clipping. This avoids its initially large gradient rescaling the unrelated primary-decoder gradients. An earlier shared-clip preflight/run is preserved in stage27_shared_clip_preliminary_attempt and excluded from the comparison.
