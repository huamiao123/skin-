Stage25: diagnose the failure before selecting an intervention

Authorization: prepare follow-up work now and serialize it after Stage24;
latest user constraint: identify a problem and test its cause before optimizing.
This stage does not train a new model and does not change previous experiments.

Established observation (exploratory, seed42 development validation):
Stage22 late-stage adaptation improves macro Dice by 0.648 percentage points,
but reduces macro recall and loses 0.964 points in the largest area quartile.
Stage23 lower encoder learning rate does not improve overall macro Dice.
These observations do not establish a lack of global information or justify
adding a boundary/fusion/attention module by themselves.

Inputs: completed Stage22, Stage23 and (after it finishes) Stage24, their
preselected best-Dice probabilities and per-image records for all three
already-exported checkpoint criteria. Only the fixed 762 development cases.
Never read held-out images, masks, predictions or test metrics.

Questions and checks fixed before the new diagnostics:
1. Is loss of lesion extent replicated in seed43 using the same area groups?
2. Are false negatives close to the 0.5 threshold or strongly background-like?
   Report probabilities <0.1, and the diagnostic global threshold curve.
   No threshold is adopted for deployment or substituted for official metrics.
3. Is spatial ordering still sufficient? Exact best per-image threshold Dice
   is an offline oracle using ground truth, not a deployable score or a bound
   on possible architectural improvements. Preserve ties in probabilities.
4. Are errors within 3/8 pixels of the annotation boundary or deeper inside?
   Distance uses zero padding outside the image; frame contact is reported.
5. Is the conclusion dependent on best-Dice selection? Also compare the
   previously exported best-loss and last checkpoints without reselecting them.
6. Does frame contact co-occur with failures? Stratify it within area quartiles;
   do not interpret this association as proof of a padding/augmentation cause.

Area cut points come from Stage22 validation GT quartiles and are reused for
all models and seeds. Each stage's probabilities are checked against the
recorded per-image TP/FP/FN/TN at 0.5, with exact validation ID order and mask
preprocessing. Diagnostics record source/input hashes and per-image outputs.

Execution: CPU-only. Completed Stage22/23 can be examined immediately;
Stage24 runs only after DONE.json exists and its training process exits.
Queue uses a lock, durable state and explicit failure/timeout detection.

Decision discipline: no automatic module training. Use the report to choose
one falsifiable intervention and its matched control. A coarse auxiliary head
is only a possible hypothesis, not an approved causal explanation or novelty.
The current decoder already combines four scales and uses Swin blocks; its
8x8 level has a window covering that feature map. Do not describe it as having
no multiscale fusion or no global context.

Limitations: development analyses follow observation of Stage22 failures;
seed43 reuses the same images, so it is seed replication, not independent-data
confirmation. Two seeds cannot estimate variability precisely. Annotation
ambiguity and unmeasured identity overlap may remain. No new SOTA claims.
